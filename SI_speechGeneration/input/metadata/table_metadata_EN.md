- speech_ID: ID of the speech
- lexical_density (the higher, the more complex): ratio of the number of content words to the total number of words
- lexical_concentration (the lower, the more complex): ratio of the number of occurrences of the 50 most frequent content lemmas to the total number of content words
- TTR (the higher, the more complex): ratio of unique tokens divided by the total number of tokens
- hapax_ratio (the higher, the more complex): ratio of the number of content lemmas that occur only once to the total number of content words
- LABEL_LEXICAL_COMPLEXITY: S (simple) or C (complex) label depending on the lexical density, concentration, type-token ratio, and hapax values
- clause_per_sentence_ratio (the higher, the more complex): ratio of the number of clauses to the number sentences
- LABEL_SYNTACTIC_COMPLEXITY: S (simple) or C (complex) label depending on the clause/sentence ratio value
- n_easy_numbers: number of numbers with 1 or 2 items when pronounced (e.g., 5 || 27 || 2000), plus number of dates (e.g., 1959 || 2007)
- n_difficult_numbers: number of numbers with 3 or more items when pronounced (e.g., 365 || 2306 || 5,500,000), plus number of decimals (e.g., 2.13)
- max_n_numbers_three_consecutive_sentences: highest number of numbers occurring in three consecutive sentences
- number_complexity_score (the higher, the more complex): sum of easy numbers, difficult numbers times two, and the maximum number of numbers in three consecutive sentences
- LABEL_NUMBERS: S (simple) or C (complex) label depending on the number complexity score
- n_rhetorical_devices (the higher, the more complex): total number of occurrences of irony, wordplay, and metaphors (live metaphors, dead metaphors, and metaphorical concepts)
- LABEL_RHETORICAL_DEVICES: S (simple) or C (complex) label depending on the total number of rhetorical devices
